Pumpkin cinnamon roll focaccia.

Soft pumpkin focaccia folded around brown sugar and cinnamon, baked in butter and glazed with honey while it's still hot. Baked today by Martin for the Salon Salon concert & potluck.

What's in it

14 ingredients

Wheat flour, pumpkin puree, water, brown sugar, butter, powdered sugar, honey, olive oil, milk, yeast, vanilla extract, cinnamon, pumpkin pie spice, salt.

The vanilla extract contains alcohol. It goes in the dough and is baked at 450°F. Recipe adapted from Humbly Homemade.
