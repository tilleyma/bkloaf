The idea

Every bake is a lot.

4 hrs. 1 loaf. Your own starter.

Brooklyn Loaf is Martin's sourdough label: small hands-on classes, bread socials and pop-ups. Brooklyn roots, not Brooklyn made — this thing might move upstate one day.

The identity borrows from a working warehouse bakery: stencilled flour sacks, rubber stamps, kraft paper, shipping labels and lot numbers. Warm tones of crust and flour, with a little grit from Red Hook and the Navy Yard.

Each class, drop and pop-up gets a lot number — LOT 24·10·26, LOT KGN-11·26 — and everything else follows from that: stamped marks, mono labels, ticket-style info and square corners.

Wet hands for every step. Keep it under 85°F. Real numbers, no promises that aren't confirmed.
